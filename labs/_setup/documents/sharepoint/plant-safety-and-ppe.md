## The short version

Eye protection and safety footwear are worn everywhere on the shop floor, by everyone, at all times.
Everything below is in addition to that.

## By area

| Area | In addition to eye protection and safety footwear |
|---|---|
| Machining | Hearing protection. No gloves at rotating machinery. Long hair tied back |
| Cladding and Welding | Welding helmet with the correct shade, flame-resistant clothing, welding gloves, local extraction running |
| Bending | Hearing protection. Nobody inside the marked floor zone while the press is cycling |
| Coating | Respiratory protection appropriate to the coating in use, chemical-resistant gloves, extraction running |
| Assembly and Testing | Hearing protection during gas tests. Nobody inside the barrier while any assembly is pressurised |
| Offices and walkways | Eye protection and safety footwear on marked walkways crossing the shop floor |

## Pressurised testing

The test bays are barriered whenever an assembly is pressurised. While the barrier is closed:

- do not enter, for any reason, including to investigate a leak;
- do not pass tools or materials across the barrier;
- depressurise fully before approaching the assembly.

A leak found during a test is a reason to stop and depressurise, never a reason to look closer.

## Reporting

Report every injury, near miss and unsafe condition the same day, however minor. A near miss
reported today is the incident that does not happen next month.

Safety concerns are never a reason for delay that needs justifying. If something looks unsafe, stop
the work and tell the supervisor.

## Visitors and contractors

Visitors are issued eye protection and are escorted at all times on the shop floor. Contractors
working in an area follow that area's rules in full and are briefed by the area supervisor before
starting.
