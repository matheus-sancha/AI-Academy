## What this page is

A summary of the acceptance criteria Technik applies to corrosion-resistant weld overlay, gathered
in one place for engineers who need the answer quickly. It is a summary. Where it differs from the
governing work instruction, **the work instruction wins**.

| Question | Answer | Governing document |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum finished overlay thickness | 3.0 mm at every measurement point | `SWI70000318` §5.1 |
| How many thickness readings | At least 8, four at each end of the band | `SWI70000318` §5.1 |
| Porosity allowed | Max 3 indications over 0.5 mm in any 50 mm; none over 1.5 mm | `SWI70000318` §5.2 |
| Slag inclusions allowed | None | `SWI70000318` §5.2 |
| Undercut at the toe | Not permitted | `SWI70000318` §5.2 |
| Cracks | Not permitted, any size | `SWI70000318` §5.2 |
| Who can close a related notification | The quality engineer, after verification | `SOP70000101` §5 |

## External standards

Technik's criteria are derived from the relevant industry standards for subsea production equipment
and for corrosion-resistant alloy cladding. Those standards are referenced by title and number in
the project specification for each project; **their text is not reproduced on the intranet or in
Technik documents**, and copies must be obtained through the engineering document controller.

Where a project specification imposes a criterion stricter than the table above, the project
specification applies. Ask the project engineer if you are unsure which applies to the part in front
of you.

## Frequently asked

**The drawing says nothing about overlay thickness. What do I use?**
`SWI70000318` applies wherever the drawing is silent. That means 3.0 mm.

**The part has been re-prepared twice. Do I need a notification?**
Yes. `SWI70000318` §4.2 requires one where re-preparation is needed more than once on the same part.

**The acceptance criteria are changing, aren't they?**
`ECN70000042` proposes a revision to `SWI70000318` §5.2. Until the resulting revision is released,
revision B is in force. A proposed change is not a change — see `SOP70000114` §3.
