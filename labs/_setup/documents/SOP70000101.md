## 1. Purpose

This procedure defines when a Quality Notification (QN) is raised, what it must contain, how it is
progressed, and when it may be closed. Its aim is that every departure from the drawing, the work
instruction or the specification is recorded once, in one place, with enough information for someone
who was not present to understand it.

## 2. When to raise a notification

Raise a QN when any of the following occurs:

- a characteristic is measured outside the tolerance on the released drawing;
- a surface, weld or coating fails the acceptance criteria in the governing work instruction;
- material or documentation is missing, or does not match what the work order calls for;
- the same preparation or rework step is required more than once on the same part;
- a test in Assembly and Testing does not meet its acceptance criterion.

Do not raise a QN for work that is still in progress and within tolerance, and do not raise a second
notification for a defect that is already open. If in doubt whether an existing notification covers
what you have found, add to the existing one rather than creating another.

## 3. Required content

| Field | Guidance |
|---|---|
| Work order | The work order the defect was found on |
| Operation | The manufacturing operation, not the work centre |
| Part and serial number | Both, where the part is serialised |
| Defect type | From the standard list: dimensional, porosity, inclusion, undercut, crack indication, distortion, thickness, surface finish, fit-up, ovality, coating defect, leak |
| Description | What was found, where on the part, and how it was measured. State readings and the acceptance criterion they were compared against |
| Priority | Section 4 |

The description is read by planners, engineers and, increasingly, by software. Write what you
observed. Do not write instructions for the reader, and do not paste content from other systems into
the description field.

## 4. Priority

| Priority | Meaning | First response |
|---|---|---|
| 1-Critical | Safety, pressure containment, or a defect that stops a project milestone | Same working day |
| 2-High | The part cannot continue to the next operation | 2 working days |
| 3-Medium | Work may continue; disposition needed before the next operation | 5 working days |
| 4-Low | Recorded for trend analysis; no immediate impact | 10 working days |

Priority is set by the person raising the notification and may be changed only by the quality
engineer.

## 5. States

A notification moves through three states.

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Open | Raised, not yet assigned or investigated |
| In Process | Assigned, under investigation or awaiting a disposition |
| Closed | Disposition applied, verification complete, record finalised |

A notification may only be closed by the quality engineer, and only once the disposition has been
carried out and verified. Closing a notification because the work order has completed is not
permitted.

## 6. Escalation

A notification that remains Open for more than **30 calendar days** shall be reviewed at the weekly
quality meeting, and the reason for the delay recorded. Priority 1-Critical notifications are
reviewed daily until they reach In Process.
