## 1. Purpose

This instruction defines the hydrostatic and gas tests carried out on assembled subsea trees and
manifolds during Factory Acceptance Testing, and the criteria by which they pass or fail.

## 2. Safety

Hydrostatic testing is pressurised work. Before any test:

- confirm the test area is barriered and that no unauthorised person is inside the barrier;
- confirm relief settings and gauge calibration dates;
- confirm that everyone present is wearing the personal protective equipment required for the test
  bay.

Never approach a pressurised assembly to investigate a leak. Depressurise first.

## 3. Body hydrostatic test

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Test medium | Clean water with corrosion inhibitor |
| Set point | 690 bar |
| Hold | 60 minutes |
| Gauge | Calibrated, recorded by gauge identifier |
| Readings | At 0, 30 and 60 minutes as a minimum |

**Acceptance.** No visible leakage, and no pressure drop greater than **1 bar** across the hold,
after temperature stabilisation. A drop greater than 1 bar is a failure. Record every reading,
including the ambient temperature, whether the test passes or fails.

Where a test fails, raise a quality notification in accordance with `SOP70000101` before any
disassembly, and record the gauge identifier used.

## 4. Seat hydrostatic test

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Set point | 517 bar |
| Hold | 30 minutes |
| Readings | At 0, 15 and 30 minutes as a minimum |

**Acceptance.** As section 3: no visible leakage and no pressure drop greater than 1 bar.

## 5. Nitrogen seat test

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Test medium | Nitrogen |
| Set point | 103 bar |
| Hold | 15 minutes |

**Acceptance.** Zero bubbles per minute. Any measurable bubble rate is a failure.

## 6. Retest

A test may be repeated once after the cause of a failure has been identified and corrected. A
repeated test is recorded as a separate test with its own identifier; it does not replace the record
of the failed test. A second failure shall be escalated to the quality engineer and the project
engineer before any further attempt.

## 7. Records

Every test is recorded with its test identifier, the set point, the hold duration, every pressure
reading with its time, the ambient temperature and the gauge identifier. Records are retained with
the unit serial number.
