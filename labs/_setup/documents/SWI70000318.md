## 1. Purpose

This instruction defines how a surface is prepared before corrosion-resistant overlay is deposited,
and how the prepared surface and the finished overlay are inspected. It applies to all parts whose
routing includes a Cladding operation.

## 2. Scope

This instruction covers manual and automated weld overlay of nickel alloy on low-alloy steel
substrates at Plant 1 and Plant 2. It does not cover overlay repair after service, which is covered
by the repair procedure for the project concerned.

## 3. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Welding operator | Prepares the surface, deposits the overlay, records the readings required by this instruction |
| Quality engineer | Performs or witnesses the inspections in sections 4 and 5, and dispositions any non-conformance |
| Manufacturing engineer | Owns this instruction and the associated data collection points |

## 4. Surface preparation

### 4.1 Before starting

Confirm that the drawing revision on the work order paperwork matches the released revision in
Teamcenter. If it does not, stop and refer the work order to the manufacturing engineer before any
material is removed.

### 4.2 Surface preparation before overlay

Before any overlay is deposited, the prepared bore of part `P7000001042` shall be inspected
against drawing `DU700001042` at the released revision. The surface shall be free of scale, oil and
machining burrs. Surface roughness shall not exceed the value stated in the drawing for the bore
zone concerned. Where a previous overlay has been removed, the base metal shall be verified by
thickness measurement at no fewer than four points around the circumference, and the results
recorded on the data collection point sheet `DCP70000076`.

| Zone | Preparation | Inspection | Record |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bore zone 1 | Machine to drawing | Visual and roughness | DCP sheet, section 3 |
| Bore zone 2 | Machine to drawing | Visual and roughness | DCP sheet, section 3 |
| Bore zone 3 | Machine, then degrease | Visual, roughness, thickness | DCP sheet, sections 3 and 4 |

Any surface that fails inspection shall be re-prepared and re-inspected before the overlay is
started. A quality notification shall be raised where re-preparation is required more than once on
the same part.

## 5. Overlay inspection

### 5.1 Thickness

Finished overlay thickness shall be not less than **3.0 mm** at every measurement point. Measure at
no fewer than eight points: four at each end of the overlay band, evenly spaced around the
circumference. Record every reading, not only the minimum.

Where any reading is below 3.0 mm, raise a quality notification against the Cladding operation and
refer the part to the manufacturing engineer. Do not deposit additional overlay without a written
disposition.

### 5.2 Visual acceptance criteria

Examine the full overlay surface under adequate lighting after the surface has cooled.

| Feature | Acceptance |
|---|---|
| Porosity | No more than 3 indications greater than 0.5 mm in any 50 mm length, and no indication greater than 1.5 mm |
| Slag inclusion | None permitted on the overlay surface or at the overlay toe |
| Undercut at the toe | Not permitted |
| Cracks of any kind | Not permitted |

Any surface that does not meet these criteria is a non-conformance. Raise a quality notification in
accordance with `SOP70000101` and record the affected zone.

> Note: the acceptance criteria in section 5.2 are the subject of Engineering Change Notification
> `ECN70000042`. Until the resulting revision is released, the criteria above remain in force.

## 6. Records

| Record | Where |
|---|---|
| Thickness readings | `DCP70000076`, sections 3 and 4 |
| Visual inspection result | Work order confirmation |
| Non-conformances | Quality notification, per `SOP70000101` |
